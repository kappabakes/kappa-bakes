Card and wallet marks for the footer.

These are trademarks, so use the official artwork rather than a lookalike.
Each brand publishes a free asset pack:

  Visa        — usa.visa.com/run-your-business/small-business-tools/payment-technology.html
  Mastercard  — brand.mastercard.com
  Amex        — americanexpress.com/us/merchant/brand-center.html
  Apple Pay   — developer.apple.com/apple-pay/marketing/
  Google Pay  — developers.google.com/pay/api/web/guides/brand-guidelines

Save them here as:

  visa.svg
  mastercard.svg
  amex.svg
  apple-pay.svg
  google-pay.svg

Until a file is there, the footer shows a plain text chip instead — so the
site works from day one and gets smarter when you add the artwork.

Only list what your Stripe account actually accepts. Amex has to be enabled
in Stripe, and Apple Pay needs your domain verified in the Stripe dashboard
under Settings > Payment methods > Apple Pay.
